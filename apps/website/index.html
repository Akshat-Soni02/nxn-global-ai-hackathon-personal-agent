Understand

Run

It runs when you say, on a schedule, or when a file appears in a folder. Anything that sends, pays or deletes waits for your OK in the inbox. When a page changes, Shadow repairs the step, keeps the fix only with proof it worked, and lets you roll it back in one click.

Private by design

Your Mac is the server

There is no Shadow backend. The app on your Mac holds your workflows, your inbox and your memory, and calls the model with your own key.

Words, not pictures

With your consent, Shadow takes a picture at each step and reads the words on it on your Mac. Emails, phone and card numbers, keys and the words you name are masked before anything leaves. Pictures leave only if you allow that too, blacked out first.

Nothing outward without you

Steps that send, pay, publish or delete wait for your approval. A repair can never add one, turn an approval off, or reach a site you didn't use.

Gone in a day

A recording's raw files are deleted 24 hours after the workflow is saved. The workflow keeps only what replay needs.
